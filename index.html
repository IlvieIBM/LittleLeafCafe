About us

Little Leaf started in 2019 as a coffee cart outside Greenfield station. Our regulars kept asking where they could sit down, so in 2021 we moved into the old flower shop across the road — and kept the green door.

We're a team of six who care about doing simple things well: good beans, fresh bakes, and remembering your order.

Why people love us

  • Beans roasted locally every week
  • Oat, soy and almond milk at no extra cost
  • Free Wi-Fi and plenty of plugs
  • Bring a reusable cup and get 20p off
  • Dogs are always welcome

Getting here

Turn left out of the main exit of Greenfield station and look for the green door. Bike racks are right outside, and there's step-free access through the side entrance.
